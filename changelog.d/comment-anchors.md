### Added

- **A comment on a passage follows the passage.** Text typed before it moves it along, text
  typed inside it stays part of it, and edits by several people at once settle on the same
  place for everyone. If the passage is deleted altogether, the comment is not lost: it is
  marked as orphaned and stays in the page's discussion with its quote, and it does not
  come back to life if the same words are typed again later, because that would be a
  different passage.
