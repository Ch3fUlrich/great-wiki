### Added

- **A search box in the header, and a results page.** Type a word and press Enter (or press
  `/` anywhere outside a text field to jump to the box): the wiki answers with the pages,
  topics and tasks that match, in three groups, with the matched words marked in each page's
  excerpt. It works without JavaScript — the box is an ordinary form that opens
  `/suche?q=…`, an address you can send to someone. You only ever see what you may read: a
  search that matches nothing but a page you may not open looks exactly like a search that
  matches nothing at all, and no group says how many results were left out.
