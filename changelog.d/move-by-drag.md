### Added

- **Drag a page onto another in the sidebar to move it.** Dropping opens the move dialog
  on the dragged page with the new parent already chosen and the access change already
  shown — nothing moves until you press »Jetzt verschieben« there, and a move that would
  widen access you do not administer has no such button. Dropping a page onto itself, its
  own subpages or the parent it already has does nothing. The dialog and the keyboard path
  are unchanged.
