### Added

- **Comments under every page.** Anyone who can see a page sees its comment thread below the
  text; signed-in readers can add a comment, reply, and mark a thread »Erledigt« or reopen it
  (»Wieder öffnen«). Resolved threads are collapsed under »Erledigt (n)« and kept; nothing can
  be deleted. A comment that is about a passage shows the quoted text, and when that passage
  has been removed from the page it says »Textstelle nicht mehr vorhanden« and keeps the
  comment. If the comments cannot be loaded the page still opens and the panel says so.
- **Commenting on a passage while editing.** With text selected in the editor, »Kommentieren«
  attaches a comment to that passage; the anchor follows later edits.

### Known limitations

- Passage comments can only be written from the editor. Readers who cannot edit can comment on
  the page as a whole, not on a selected passage.
