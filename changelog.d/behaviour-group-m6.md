### Added

- **The end-to-end browser checks now walk comments and notifications.** A new group invites
  three people through the real acceptance page and checks, in a browser, that a mention
  gives the reader a badge and a list entry that clears with »Alle als gelesen markieren«,
  that a read-only reader can reply and the author hears of it, that a resolved thread is
  collapsed under »Erledigt (n)«, survives a reload and has no delete control, that someone
  without access to a page sees the same 404, an empty inbox and no badge as for a page that
  does not exist, and that a comment on a selected passage is kept, marked »Textstelle nicht
  mehr vorhanden«, once the passage is deleted.
