### Added

- **Groundwork for notifications: an event record that cannot outlive your access.** Things
  that happen to you on a page (a reply, a mention, an assignment, an edit by someone else)
  are recorded as who might hear of them, and checked again whenever you look. If your
  access to the page is withdrawn afterwards, the entry disappears from your list and your
  unread count together, and nothing says that something was hidden. No page text is kept
  in the record; titles are read from the page as it is now. Nothing shows this yet.
